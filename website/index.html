The film

Meet Index.

Fifty-five seconds on what BuzzCloud Index does, and why we made it.

Privacy

Private
by default.

Ads and trackers are stopped before they ever load, using the same filter lists trusted by millions. Pages come out cleaner, lighter and faster.

  • Built-in ad & tracker blocking, on from the start
  • Every site is asked not to track or sell your data
  • Camera, microphone & location only when you allow
Private windows

Leaves no trace.

Open a private window and nothing is kept. No history, and every cookie and bit of site data is erased the moment you close it.

  • Nothing saved to your history
  • Cookies & site data wiped on close
  • One shortcut away: Ctrl + Shift + N
Simplicity

Simple, as it should be.

One bar to search or go anywhere. Tabs, bookmarks and history right where you expect them, and nothing you don't need.

  • Search or type an address in the same place
  • Bookmarks on every new tab
  • The keyboard shortcuts you already know
Design

Designed, not decorated.

Quiet typography, light, dark and private themes, and the sky on every new tab. A browser that gets out of the way of the web.

  • Follows your system's light or dark mode
  • Automatic updates, quietly in the background
  • Built on Chromium, so every site just works
